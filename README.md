# Lobotomy

Lobotomy is a satirical web project that parodies the modern internet's obsession with advertising, engagement, and attention. An endless stream of absurd advertisements, questionable offers, clickbait, and digital noise — all packed into one place. The goal is to exaggerate the things we've grown accustomed to online and turn them into an experience of deliberate chaos.

The modern internet competes for every second of your attention. Lobotomy takes that idea to its logical extreme. There is no meaningful product to buy, no subscription to sign up for, and no real offer to accept. Just an exaggerated reflection of the digital environment we experience every day.

[Live Demo](https://aksond1.github.io/Lobotomy/)

## Features

- **Chaotic feed** — an unpredictable arrangement of advertisements.
- **Fictional advertisements** — absurd products, ridiculous offers, and questionable promises.
- **Theme switching** — change the visual appearance.
- **Shuffle** — randomize the content and embrace the chaos.
- **About** — learn more about the project.
